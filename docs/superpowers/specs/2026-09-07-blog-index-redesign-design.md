# Blog index redesign — design spec

**Status:** APPLIED 2026-09-07 (owner-approved) — in the working tree, not yet committed.
`blog/index.html` is regenerated by `scripts/reindex_blog.py` (idempotent); both linters carry
the reading-order chain rule; `build_series.py --index-fragment` emits the new shape
byte-identical to the shipped sections; `feed.xml` regenerated; docs updated.
**Date:** 2026-09-07 · **Target:** `blog/index.html` (the only file whose *design* changes)

## 1. Why

The blog index has grown from 37 posts in two series to 83 posts in six series without its
layout changing. Measured on 2026-09-07 (Chrome, local server, `blog/index.html` at `f0decd6`):

| Fact | Desktop 1440×900 | Phone 390×844 |
|---|---|---|
| Page height | 20,097 px = **22.3 viewports** | 22,824 px = **27.0 viewports** |
| First post card | y = 1,088 (188 px *below* the fold) | y = 1,536 (1.8 viewports down) |
| Posts visible in the first screen | **0** | **0** |
| Jump-chip strip | 2 rows | **6 rows, 255 px**, centre-ragged |
| Share of page that is one uniform card grid | 86.7 % | ~85 % |

Every one of the 83 cards repeats the same avatar, the same byline, a "Read →" label and a
hidden third tag; the six series are numbered curricula, yet **no card shows its number**, and
five of the six sections render newest-first (post #7, #20, #10, #7, #24 lead their sections)
while the sixth reads #1 → #9. Chip icons and section icons disagree (⚙️ vs 🚀), the card
title wraps the English half of every title in `lang="th"`, and the page has no gutter below
1200 px. None of this is a defect of the visual system, which is coherent and worth keeping
(Sunrise hero, house tokens, drawn covers). It is a density and wayfinding problem.

Owner brief: *"make it more tidy and easy to access, good UX/UI."*

## 2. Goals and non-goals

**Goals**

1. A first screen that says what this is, who writes it, and where to start — with real
   content reachable without scrolling.
2. Every series legible as a *course*: numbered, in reading order, with the whole archive still
   on one page (the linters require every post to be carded here).
3. Series navigation available at any scroll depth, zero JavaScript.
4. Fewer, quieter, better-aligned elements per post; correct language-of-parts markup.
5. Every string the repo's tooling reads by regex is preserved exactly (§6).

**Non-goals (deliberately)**

- No per-series landing pages (a second phase, see §9).
- No new hero gradient, token, typeface, or type-scale change. INV-28 and the page-design
  skill fix those.
- No change to the shared 10-page nav chrome block (burger size, Scholar CTA, backdrop-filter):
  those are a separate sitewide sweep.
- No JavaScript, ever (INV-38).

## 3. Research that shaped the design

- **NN/g** names blog listings as the case where cards lose to lists: homogeneous items scan
  faster as a vertical list with fixed element positions; irregular card grids cause semi-random
  scanning and de-emphasise ranking. Attention decays hard down a page (>42 % of viewing time
  in the top 20 %), so series 5–6 of a 22-viewport page are effectively invisible.
- **Strong personal/engineering blogs are hybrid** (jvns.ca, joshwcomeau.com, overreacted.io,
  gwern.net/index, stripe.com/blog): a small curated tier on top, then dense topic-grouped rows;
  none shows an author on index items.
- **Sticky in-page navigation** measurably speeds navigation on long content pages (NN/g via
  Smashing, ~22 %), provided the bar stays small and `scroll-padding-top` covers it (WCAG 2.4.11).
- **Pure-CSS "filters"** (`:target` tabs, checkbox hacks) fail accessibility reviews; the
  accessible zero-JS filter is *navigation*: anchors to sections now, real series pages later.
- **Thai typography** needs ~10–15 % more leading than Latin (≥ 1.55) for stacked vowels and
  tone marks; mixed "EN — TH" strings on one line overshoot the 50–75-character measure, which
  is what produced the three-line titles and six different card heights.
- **House vocabulary already has the answer**: the phone row-card this page ships at ≤768 px
  (96 px square thumb, clamped excerpt, byline hidden) is the `card--row` idiom `news/` and
  `publications/` use. The redesign promotes it to every width.

### 4.1 Row card anatomy (desktop; phone is the same with a 64 px thumb)

```html
<a href="git-branching.html" class="card">
  <span class="card__num">01</span>
  <div class="card__image">
    <img src="../images/git-branching-cover.jpg" alt="" width="800" height="800" loading="lazy" decoding="async">
  </div>
  <div class="card__body">
    <h3 class="card__title"><span class="card__en">Git Branching</span><span class="card__sep"> — </span><span class="card__th" lang="th">…</span></h3>
    <p class="card__excerpt"><span lang="th">…</span></p>
    <p class="card__meta"><time datetime="2026-03-07">7 Mar 2026</time> · 12 min read</p>
  </div>
</a>
```

- The **ordinal** is gold-dark tabular figures, zero-padded, right-aligned in a 2 rem gutter
  and part of the link's accessible name; the six unnumbered OpenClaw posts render an empty
  gutter under a "Standalone posts" label.
- The **title** is one `<h3>` whose *text* is unchanged ("EN — TH"), so `gen_feed.py` and
  INV-10 see the same string; the separator span is `display:none`, the TH span is a block at
  0.86 rem / 1.6 leading. The English half is no longer inside `lang="th"`.
- The **excerpt** is clamped to one line with an ellipsis (with a small bottom padding so a
  clipped Thai line keeps its lower vowels); the **meta** line keeps the `<time datetime>`
  INV-36 reads and the read time.
- **Removed from every row:** avatar, author name, tag row, "Read →". The whole row is still
  one `<a>` (one tab stop, one link-list entry, one large target).
- **Hover / focus:** border and shadow only, title turns blue; no lift, no image zoom.
  `:visited` titles turn slate-light, a read/unread cue with no JavaScript.
- Two columns at ≥ 992 px (`minmax(460px, 1fr)`), one column below.

### 4.2 What the reader gets

| Question | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| What is this, whose is it? | title + topic list; author only in the nav logo | identity line + one sentence + counts |
| Where do I start? | a spotlight with no rank, then post #7 | "Start here" spotlight, then row 01 of every series |
| Where is post #5? | open cards one by one | read the number |
| Change series mid-page | scroll back ≤ 19,000 px | sticky bar, chip lights up after a jump (`:has(:target)`) |
| Have I read this? | nothing | visited titles go quiet |
| Screen reader | "HERMES DESKTOP Safety … Anirach Mingkhwan 7 Sep 2026 18 min read Read →" ×83, English voiced in Thai | title-led names, ~200 chars, correct `lang` per fragment |

## 5. Decisions and alternatives

| Decision | Chosen | Rejected, and why |
|---|---|---|
| Item shape | numbered row cards, two columns | 3-col tall cards (the problem); jvns-style text-only rows (throws away the drawn-cover system); 4-col small cards (still a wall) |
| Order within a series | **reading order, #1 first** | newest-first with descending numbers — legible, but a curriculum that starts at the bottom; and four of six series were published whole on one day, so "newest within series" means nothing |
| Card order ⇄ DevOps chain | card order **is** the chain (`devops_chain()` returns it un-reversed) | keep `reversed()` and keep DevOps newest-first while the other five read forward — the inconsistency the critique flagged |
| Series bar | sticky, one row, text chips, `--blue-light` 1 px border (3.2:1 edge), opaque `--bg` ground | in-hero only (gone after one flick); duplicated per section (six copies to keep honest); `backdrop-filter` (repainted every frame on a phone) |
| Chip emoji | none | INV-02f reads chip text with `[^<]*`, so an `aria-hidden` glyph is impossible; a bare glyph is announced |
| Title emoji (45 titles) | stripped on the index | two card species per section; `norm_title` strips them anyway; the post `<h1>` keeps its emoji |
| Tags | removed from rows | 149 tags, 122 singletons — decorative, not a facet; the first tag repeated the section name |
| Spotlight | kept, `class="feature"`, `h2` kicker + `h3` title, eyebrow with date/series/position | dropping it (loses the LCP image and the only "editor's pick" tier); `h2` title (a lone article among six sections in the heading list) |
| Descriptions | one EN sentence + one TH sentence per series; the "bilingual" boilerplate moves to the hero | 125–190 px of prose per section, repeated in 4/6 |
| Phone density | 64 px thumb, one-line excerpt, spotlight image+title only | hiding thumbs (loses the cover language); excerpt hidden (the only per-post description) |
| `content-visibility: auto` | **not used** | Safari find-in-page can skip that content; Cmd-F is how readers find a post in an 83-row list |
| Per-series pages | phase 2 | needs new URLs, sitemap/llms/feed decisions, and a linter model change; not needed to fix density |

## 6. Markup contract — preserved exactly

Five regex parsers read this file (`check_site.py` ×3 copies of the card regex, `gen_feed.py`,
`verify-wiring.py`, plus `check_visibility.py`). The prototype keeps every string they read:

| String | Reader | Kept as |
|---|---|---|
| `<a href="slug.html" class="card">` single space, href first, no extra class | RE_CARD ×5 | unchanged — variants live on the container, never on the card |
| first `<img src=…>` in a card is the cover | INV-07b | unchanged (the avatar `<img>` is gone) |
| `<h[1-6] class="card__title">…</h…>` no other attribute | 3 parsers | `<h3>`; inner spans stripped by `text_of`/`norm_title` |
| `<p class="card__excerpt">` bare | gen_feed | unchanged |
| `<time datetime="YYYY-MM-DD">` inside the card | INV-36 | in `.card__meta` |
| `<section class="series-section" id="…">` | RE_SECTION | unchanged ids |
| `<h2 class="series-title">…</h2>` verbatim in llms.txt | check_visibility L2 | unchanged |
| `<span class="series-count">N articles</span>` | INV-02c, `--fix` | unchanged |
| `<span class="blog-hero__stat"><strong>N</strong> Label</span>` | INV-02a/b, `--fix` | unchanged, restyled |
| `<nav class="blog-jump"…>` chips `<a href="#id">text · N</a>` | INV-02f | text shortened, no emoji, `· N` kept |
| first `<nav>` is the site nav | INV-23 | unchanged |
| `class="feature"` spotlight | comment + counters | unchanged |
| social block, canonical, `og:image` size | INV-27 | unchanged (descriptions rewritten) |
| `<script type="application/ld+json">` only | INV-38 | one `Blog` block added |

Result on the prototype tree: `check_site.py` **61/61 clean, 0 violations**;
`verify-wiring.py` **CLEAN**; `check_visibility.py --strict` **99 / AI-READY**, with the one
intended linter change applied (§7 step 4).

## 7. Implementation plan

The generator that built the prototype is the migration tool: it parses the current index for
every card and re-emits the page, so nothing is retyped by hand.

1. **Land the generator** as `scripts/reindex_blog.py` (from the prototype's `build_index.py`),
   run it once: `python3 scripts/reindex_blog.py --repo . --out blog/index.html`.
2. **`scripts/build_series.py` `index_fragment()`** emits the new card shape (ordinal from
   `p["n"]`, EN/TH split, group labels from `man["groups"]`, manifest order, emoji-free chip).
   Two generated series (27 posts) depend on it.
3. **Regenerate** `feed.xml` (`scripts/gen_feed.py` — 45 titles lose their trailing emoji) and
   `sitemap.xml` (`scripts/gen_sitemap.py`, in the following commit so the git date exists).
4. **Chain rule:** `check_site.py` `devops_chain()` and `verify-wiring.py` line ~185 return the
   `#series-devops` card order **un-reversed**. Docs that state the old rule are rewritten in
   the same commit: `CLAUDE.md` ("Card order IS the prev/next chain", "newest post at the TOP"),
   `blog-post/SKILL.md` steps 5–7, `site-check/SKILL.md` INV-04e and `references/adding-a-post.md`,
   `page-design/references/components.md` §4 (card markup), `a11y-perf/SKILL.md` R1/R6 numbers.
   New DevOps posts are appended at the **end** of the section.
5. **Verification** (all from the repo root, before pushing):
   `python3 .claude/skills/site-check/scripts/check_site.py` → exit 0, `0 new, 0 known`;
   `python3 .claude/skills/blog-post/assets/verify-wiring.py` → CLEAN;
   `python3 scripts/check_visibility.py --strict` → exit 0;
   `python3 scripts/gen_feed.py --check` / `gen_sitemap.py --check` → no drift;
   `python3 scripts/build_series.py --index-fragment` output diffed against the live section;
   browser: 1440 and 390 screenshots, keyboard walk (skip link → bar → spotlight → row 01),
   a chip jump lands under the sticky bar, `:visited` visible after opening a post.
6. Commit as one change ("Blog index: numbered row cards in reading order, sticky series bar"),
   push, then `curl -s -o /dev/null -w '%{http_code}' https://anirach.com/blog/?cb=$RANDOM`.

## 9. Follow-ups (not in this change)

- **Thumbnail derivatives** (`images/<slug>-cover-160.jpg`, ~5 KB each) so a 64–72 px thumb does
  not pull an 800 px cover; needs `make_cover.py` to emit them and INV-06a/07a/35 read first.
- **Per-series pages** (`blog/series-<name>.html`, flat so `gen_sitemap.py`'s non-recursive glob
  sees them) with the series in full and a 4–6-row preview on the index.
- **Post back-links** to `./#series-<id>` so leaving a post lands in its own section.
- **Shared nav chrome sweep** (10 pages): 44 px burger, Scholar CTA inside the menu on phones,
  `aria-label`/`aria-current` on all nine `class="active"` links.
- **Card read-time drift**: 10+ cards disagree with the post hero (all 7 Hermes Desktop cards
  understate); a data fix in the posts or the manifest, not the index.

## 11. Review panel — run, and its findings applied

Five independent reviewers (design director with Nielsen scoring, WCAG 2.2 reviewer with
browser access, markup-contract verifier, phone/performance critic, and an auditor of the
original 54-finding critique backlog) reviewed the first prototype: 59 findings, 2 blockers.
The backlog audit scored the prototype **36 resolved / 11 partial / 4 declined** against the
original critique. All blockers and majors were fixed and re-verified in a second render pass:

- **Doubled scroll offset** (`scroll-padding-top` + `scroll-margin-top` are additive): chip
  jumps landed 272 px down with a full row of the previous series under the bar. The margin
  rule is gone; jumps now land 15 px under the bar. The same 5rem+5rem doubling exists on the
  live page and in every post — noted as a sitewide follow-up.
- **769–990 px band**: the chip bar wrapped to a 100 px two-row sticky block. It becomes a
  left-aligned scroller at ≤1024 px with a right-edge fade and scroll-snap as the overflow
  affordance.
- **Four invalid group labels** (`</p></span>`) fixed in the generator.
- **Ordinals were `aria-hidden`** on all rows — the series position was invisible to the very
  users the finding was written for. Now audible (77 rows; the 6 unnumbered OpenClaw
  standalones keep an empty hidden span).
- **Language of parts stragglers**: the Obsidian title (Thai inside the EN span) and six
  OpenClaw excerpts (English lists wrapped in `lang="th"`) are now split correctly; one
  leftover title emoji (⏱) stripped.
- **RSS chip border** and the `:target` underline moved from `--gold` (2.2:1) to
  `--gold-dark` (5.6:1) for the 3:1 non-text bar.
- **`:visited` vs `:hover` order** fixed so a read row still lights on hover/focus.
- **Skip link** now lands on content: the series bar sits between the hero and `<main>`.
- **Phone**: in-page jumps scroll instantly at ≤768 px (a smooth 13,000 px scroll dragged
  ~3 MB of covers through the lazy-load margin); the one-line excerpt is hidden at ≤600 px
  (it mostly restated the Thai title); the count pill folds into the sticky bar at ≤600 px;
  the description clamp that truncated the Thai sentence is gone; footer links got real
  padding. Result: 11,407 px, 13.5 viewports, ~88–125 px rows.
- **Spotlight** got section rhythm (hairline + spacing) so it reads as its own tier.

Reviewer verdicts: "a clear improvement, not a marginal one" (design director, Nielsen 23/32
Good for a listing page); "clear improvement on the tooling contract — every load-bearing
regex reads the same numbers off it as off the live page" (contracts, after the one intended
`devops_chain()` change); "clear improvement on the phone" (mobile critic); "36 of 54 backlog
findings resolved outright, should proceed" (backlog auditor).

**Declined reviewer suggestions, deliberately:** section `aria-labelledby` landmarks (needs
RE_SECTION widened in the same commit — queued with the follow-ups); letting the fixed nav
scroll away on phones (would fork the shared 10-page chrome); `content-visibility: auto`
(Safari find-in-page can skip content; Cmd-F is how an 83-row list is searched); font
self-hosting and swap-CLS metric overrides (site-wide decision, not this page's).
