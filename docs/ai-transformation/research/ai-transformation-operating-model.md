### The federated model (p26), verbatim
> AI transformation cannot be sustained by a center of excellence that builds everything, nor by unrestricted domain teams selecting their own models, data, and controls. A federated operating model combines enterprise foundations with domain accountability. The center supplies policy, identity, model access, evaluation infrastructure, reusable controls, observability, and portfolio visibility. Domain teams own business meaning, workflow design, data quality, adoption, and outcomes. Independent risk functions challenge high-consequence claims and approve exceptions.

### Products and decisions, and property-specific accountability (p26), verbatim
> Organize around products and decisions rather than temporary projects. Every AI product needs a product owner, domain owner, technical owner, data-product owner, operations owner, and named owners for security, privacy, and release. Accountability is property specific. The platform can guarantee that every tool call passes an authorization service, but it cannot own whether a refund-policy answer is substantively correct. The domain owner carries that obligation. Conversely, a domain expert cannot declare an effect path secure without platform and security evidence.

### The CX-REFUND-01 roster (p27), verbatim
> In CX-REFUND-01, Customer Experience owns the outcome, workflow, and adoption. The policy owner approves meaning, thresholds, and high-severity cases. The data owner publishes the corpus. The AI platform provides context assembly, model routing, trace storage, evaluator integration, and the execution guard. Payments owns the refund API and transaction controls. Security owns the threat model and adaptive testing. Customer Operations staffs the escalation queue. A release owner verifies that these contributions resolve to one approved manifest.

### The four verbs and the portfolio council (p27), verbatim
> A practical decision-rights model separates four verbs. **Set standards** is typically central for identity, security, manifest, trace, and evaluation protocols. **Build** is shared between platform and domain product teams. **Approve** belongs to authorized business, risk, legal, and release roles based on property and consequence. **Operate** belongs to the team that can monitor, support, contain, and improve the live service. An executive portfolio council allocates capability funding, accepts residual risk at its proper level, and decides when an initiative stops.

### ★ Five operating principles (p27) — quote exactly ONE in the 💡 blockquote, list the rest in prose. Never renumber.
1. **Centralize foundations not every decision** Shared rails should be enterprise capabilities.
2. **Keep meaning and outcomes in the domain** Definitions acceptance and benefits remain local accountabilities.
3. **Assign one owner per property** Committees advise but an assurance claim needs a name.
4. **Separate creation from challenge for high stakes** Independent scrutiny tests consequential claims and exceptions.
5. **Federate learning through reusable assets** Incidents controls schemas and cases improve the platform without erasing context.

2. **ให้ Domain เป็นเจ้าของความหมายและผลลัพธ์** คำนิยาม Acceptance และ Benefit อยู่กับเจ้าของงาน

## 7. Do not assert

Things searched for and not verifiable, or verifiable but out of scope. A writer who wants any of these must send
it back for a new research pass.

1. **ISO/IEC 42001 does not, on any free source, say "roles, responsibilities, management review."**
   Re-verified 2026-09-05 by string-searching the live DOM of `iso.org/standard/42001`: *management review*,
   *roles* and *responsibilities* all return **NOT PRESENT**. The spec's research-target line asks [11] to
   support *roles, responsibilities, management review*; **it cannot, from any free ISO source.** Those are
   Harmonized-Structure clause titles behind ISO's paywall and were not read. What ISO publishes free, and
   what the post may use, is exactly two sentences from [3] plus one from [9]:
   - [3] ISO/IEC 42001 "specifies requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS) within organizations";
   - [3] an AI management system "is a set of interrelated or interacting elements of an organization intended to establish policies and objectives, as well as processes to achieve those objectives, in relation to the responsible development, provision or use of AI systems";
   - [9] "As a management system standard, ISO/IEC 42001 is built around a 'Plan-Do-Check-Act' process of establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving artificial intelligence."
   **CORRECTION, 2026-09-05:** an earlier draft of this ledger attributed to [3] the string *"putting in place
   policies and procedures for the sound governance of an organization in relation to AI, using the
   Plan-Do-Check-Act methodology."* That string is **NOT on `iso.org/standard/42001`** and no ISO page carrying
   it was found. **Do not use it.** If PDCA is wanted, use the [9] sentence with the [9] URL.
   Otherwise attribute the roles/responsibility framing to the **book's** D.3 description of [11], never to ISO.
2. **Never write that ISO/IEC 42001 certifies, guarantees or proves anything about a system**, or that a
   federated operating model "complies with" 42001. The book's boundary is explicit: public description only,
   no certification conferred.
3. **The ETDA guideline requires nothing.** Its own front matter says non-compliance, or partial adoption, is
   not a breach of law or regulation. Present it as a Thai *reference frame* for the roster — which is exactly
   what the spec's `.alert info` box asks for — never as an obligation.
4. **"Cross-functional responsibility" is not ETDA's phrase.** The guideline says the organization should define
   *"หน้าที่ (Role & Responsibility) ของผู้ที่เกี่ยวข้องกับการประยุกต์ใช้ AI ในองค์กรอย่างชัดเจนและครอบคลุมทุกมิติ"* and should assign duties to

6. **The OECD AI Principles are not law.** OECD/LEGAL/0449 is a Council **Recommendation** — voluntary. Label it
