### E12 — Failure patterns, verbatim, one line per chapter (the §7 consolidated list is an **editorial selection of ten** from these)

- **Ch1 (learning system):** "Pilot theatre, dashboards without a decision owner, averages that erase weak groups, repeated prompt patching without workflow diagnosis, learning only after incidents, and explanations that dismiss frontline feedback as user error."
- **Ch2 (maturity):** "Counting pilots as maturity, forcing every workflow toward autonomy, skipping from sandbox to integration, adding governance after deployment, treating training attendance as competence, and retaining a maturity label after evidence decays."
- **Ch3 (portfolio):** "Ranking only by projected savings, assuming the label copilot implies low authority, confusing business criticality with model indispensability, using one risk score for incomparable harms, ignoring shared-model concentration, and allowing pilots to persist without a scale-or-stop decision."
- **Ch4 (human–AI workflow):** "Automating the old process unchanged, making one exhausted expert the universal reviewer, rubber-stamp approval, hiding uncertainty behind fluent prose, allowing tools before authorization, removing entry-level work without a new learning path, and measuring local speed while downstream rework rises."
- **Ch5 (factory):** "Building a data lake without a decision, calling pilots a factory, shadow corpora, unversioned prompts, measuring uptime while ignoring semantic quality, treating all user feedback as truth, optimizing retrieval without post-state checks, and centralizing every delivery choice in one specialist queue."
- **Ch6 (operating model):** "The center-of-excellence bottleneck, uncontrolled local tools, RACI charts where everyone is consulted and nobody decides, governance arriving before launch, risk teams approving adjectives instead of evidence, platform metrics disconnected from value, and a human-in-the-loop queue with no staffing."
