## 0. What the book actually says (verbatim extraction)

### 0.1 Chapter 10 — the chapter argument (printed p. 43, opening line)

> Law defines obligations ethics addresses the remaining gap and governance turns both into decisions evidence and remedy.

> Governance is the system by which an organization decides what AI may do, who authorizes it, what evidence is required, and who acts when results are contested or harmful. A committee without decision rights is not governance. A policy without inventory, controls, and monitoring is not governance either.

### 0.2 The supplier paragraph — the post's spine (printed p. 44, verbatim)

> Govern suppliers through contracts covering data use, customer-data training, model and subprocessor changes, evaluation evidence, logs, security tests, incident notice, audit support, continuity, intellectual property, and exit. **Outsourcing technology does not outsource accountability.**

Thai mirror (คู่มือภาษาไทย, printed p. 45, verbatim):

> กำกับ Supplier ผ่านสัญญาที่ครอบคลุม Data Use การฝึกด้วยข้อมูลลูกค้า การเปลี่ยน Model หรือ Subprocessor, Evaluation Evidence, Log, Security Test, Incident Notice, Audit Support, Continuity, IP และ Exit การจ้างเทคโนโลยีไม่ใช่การโอนความรับผิด

### 0.3 The sustainability paragraph (printed p. 44, verbatim)

> Sustainability belongs inside the business case. The IEA's 2026 update on energy and AI estimates data centers used about 485 TWh in 2025 and projects roughly 950 TWh in 2030, around 3 percent of global electricity demand in that year. These are uncertain sector scenarios, not the meter for one use case. Organizations should measure total energy, emissions where estimable, water where material, hardware lifecycle, and rebound when lower unit cost drives far more use. Route tasks to the smallest model that meets quality and risk requirements and compare footprint per successful outcome rather than token alone.

Thai mirror (printed p. 45, verbatim):

> Sustainability ต้องอยู่ใน Business Case ข้อมูลปรับปรุงปี 2026 ของ IEA ประเมินว่า Data Center ใช้ไฟราว 485 TWh ในปี 2025 และอาจเพิ่มเป็นประมาณ 950 TWh ในปี 2030 หรือราว 3 เปอร์เซ็นต์ของความต้องการไฟฟ้าโลกในปีนั้น ตัวเลขนี้เป็น Scenario ของภาค ไม่ใช่มิเตอร์ของ Use Case เดียว องค์กรควรวัดพลังงานรวม Emission เท่าที่ประเมินได้ น้ำเมื่อสำคัญ Hardware Lifecycle และ Rebound เมื่อราคาต่อหน่วยลดแต่ Usage เพิ่ม ใช้โมเดลเล็กที่สุดที่ผ่านเกณฑ์และเปรียบเทียบ Footprint ต่อ Successful Outcome

### 0.4 The proportional-control paragraph (printed p. 44, verbatim)

> Apply proportional control. Low-impact drafting does not require the same gate as employment, credit, health, education, or safety decisions, but still needs inventory, data rules, supplier control, security, and user guidance. High-impact systems require impact assessment, independent challenge, meaningful human authority, appeal and remedy, and explicit residual-risk acceptance.

### 0.5 The frameworks paragraph (printed pp. 43–44, verbatim) — attribution wording is load-bearing

> Several frameworks can share evidence without being treated as interchangeable. NIST AI RMF supplies lifecycle functions. ISO IEC 42001 specifies an AI management system, while ISO IEC 42005 addresses lifecycle impact assessment. The OECD AI Principles emphasize human rights, transparency, robustness, accountability, traceability, and ongoing risk management. The ASEAN Guide and its generative AI expansion provide regional guidance. Thailand's ETDA Generative AI guideline emphasizes governance structure, strategy, operations, human oversight, and incident reporting.

Thai mirror (printed p. 45, verbatim):

> หลายกรอบใช้หลักฐานร่วมกันได้แต่ไม่ใช่สิ่งเดียวกัน NIST AI RMF ให้ Lifecycle Function ISO IEC 42001 กำหนด AI Management System ส่วน ISO IEC 42005 เน้น Impact Assessment ตลอดวงจร OECD AI Principles เน้นสิทธิมนุษยชน Transparency, Robustness, Accountability, Traceability และการบริหารความเสี่ยงต่อเนื่อง

### 0.6 The legal-status paragraph as the book has it (printed p. 44, verbatim)

> As of this edition, Thailand's dedicated AI regulation remains in development. Applicable laws such as the Personal Data Protection Act and sector rules still govern AI-related processing and decisions. The UNESCO Thailand readiness assessment identifies policy-coherence, coordination, and capability gaps. In the European Union, the AI Act is in force and progressively applicable. Official Commission guidance says enforcement powers and transparency duties apply from 2 August 2026, while 2026 changes extend key high-risk deadlines. Role and system classification require qualified legal analysis.

### 0.7 Chapter 10 — the five operating principles (verbatim, never renumbered)

1. **Maintain a live inventory and named owner** Governance begins with knowing what exists.
2. **Map obligations to evidence** Reuse artifacts responsibly without claiming frameworks are identical.
3. **Use law as the floor and ethics for the gap** Define prohibited uses affected groups and remedy.
4. **Make supplier change and exit governable** Vendor dependence is part of the risk perimeter.
5. **Measure lifecycle sustainability and rebound** Unit efficiency can coexist with rising total impact.
