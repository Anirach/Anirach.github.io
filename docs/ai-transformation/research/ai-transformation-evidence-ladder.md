## 2. Number cards

*Rule: no card, no number. Each card's Thai and English sentence is the form the number may take in the post. Every one carries denominator, comparison, setting and the book's own Boundary line.*

### N1 — 5,179 support agents
- **Value / unit:** 5,179 · customer-support agents
- **Denominator / population:** the full agent population studied in **one firm's** staggered rollout
- **Comparison:** staggered introduction, agents with tool access vs agents not yet given access
- **Task / setting:** live customer-support conversations, one company, one workflow, one deployment period (data through 2021 vintage of the tool)
- **Source:** [26] NBER WP 31161 — verbatim: *"we study the staggered introduction of a generative AI-based conversational assistant using data from 5,179 customer support agents"*
- **Book's Boundary line (verbatim, D.3 §26):** *"one firm, workflow, deployment, and period; not a universal productivity or return estimate."*
- **TH:** งานภาคสนามกับพนักงานบริการลูกค้า 5,179 คนในบริษัทเดียว รายงานว่าจำนวนเรื่องที่ปิดได้ต่อชั่วโมงเพิ่มขึ้นเฉลี่ย 14 เปอร์เซ็นต์ — เป็นตัวเลขของบริษัทหนึ่ง กระบวนงานหนึ่ง และช่วงเวลาหนึ่ง ไม่ใช่ค่าประมาณผลิตภาพสากล
- **EN:** A field deployment covering 5,179 customer-support agents at a single firm reported a 14 percent average rise in issues resolved per hour — one firm, one workflow, one period, not a universal productivity estimate.
- ⚠️ **Pair with dated status D1** (the peer-reviewed version reports 5,172).

### N2 — +14% average issues resolved per hour
- **Value / unit:** +14 percent · issues resolved per hour
- **Denominator / population:** average across the 5,179 agents of N1
- **Comparison:** with tool access vs without, within the staggered rollout
- **Task / setting:** as N1
- **Source:** [26] — verbatim: *"Access to the tool increases productivity, as measured by issues resolved per hour, by 14% on average"*
- **Boundary (verbatim, D.3 §26):** *"one firm, workflow, deployment, and period; not a universal productivity or return estimate."*
- **TH:** ค่าเฉลี่ยเพิ่มขึ้น 14 เปอร์เซ็นต์ วัดจากจำนวนเรื่องที่ปิดได้ต่อชั่วโมง เทียบระหว่างช่วงที่ได้ใช้เครื่องมือกับช่วงที่ยังไม่ได้ใช้ ในพนักงาน 5,179 คนของบริษัทเดียว
- **EN:** The 14 percent is an average — issues resolved per hour, with access versus without, across those 5,179 agents in that one firm.
- ⚠️ **Pair with D1** (the peer-reviewed version reports 15%).

### N3 — +34% for novice and low-skilled workers
- **Value / unit:** +34 percent · issues resolved per hour
- **Denominator / population:** the novice / lower-skilled subgroup **within** the same 5,179 agents (the paper does not publish the subgroup's own n in the abstract — **do not state one**)
- **Comparison:** the same subgroup with access vs without; contrasted against "minimal impact on experienced and highly skilled workers"
- **Task / setting:** as N1
- **Source:** [26] — verbatim: *"including a 34% improvement for novice and low-skilled workers but with minimal impact on experienced and highly skilled workers"*
- **Boundary (verbatim, D.3 §26):** *"one firm, workflow, deployment, and period; not a universal productivity or return estimate."*
- **TH:** กลุ่มประสบการณ์น้อยและทักษะต่ำเพิ่มขึ้น 34 เปอร์เซ็นต์ ขณะที่กลุ่มเชี่ยวชาญสูงแทบไม่เปลี่ยน — นี่คือเหตุผลที่ค่าเฉลี่ยตัวเดียวใช้ออกแบบ workflow ให้ทุกคนไม่ได้
- **EN:** The novice and lower-skilled group improved by 34 percent while the most experienced group changed minimally — which is precisely why one average cannot design one workflow for everyone.
- ⚠️ **Pair with D1.** The 34 percent figure is **not in the peer-reviewed QJE abstract**; attribute it to [26] only.

### N4 — 758 consultants
- **Value / unit:** 758 · knowledge workers (management consultants)
- **Denominator / population:** all participants in the preregistered experiment, drawn from Boston Consulting Group
- **Comparison:** randomised to three arms — no AI access · GPT-4 access · GPT-4 access plus a prompt-engineering overview — after a baseline on similar tasks
- **Task / setting:** realistic management-consulting tasks built with BCG; one model generation (GPT-4); simulated, not client-billed work
- **Source:** [28] — verbatim: *"The preregistered experiment involved 758 knowledge workers."*
- **Boundary (verbatim, D.3 §28):** *"simulated consulting tasks, one participant population, and one model generation; the frontier moves."*
- **TH:** การทดลองแบบลงทะเบียนล่วงหน้ากับที่ปรึกษา 758 คน สุ่มเข้าสามกลุ่ม — ไม่ใช้ AI ใช้ GPT-4 และใช้ GPT-4 พร้อมคำแนะนำการเขียน prompt — บนงานที่ปรึกษาจำลอง ไม่ใช่งานลูกค้าจริง และเป็นโมเดลรุ่นเดียว
- **EN:** A preregistered experiment with 758 consultants randomised them across three arms — no AI, GPT-4, and GPT-4 with a prompt-engineering overview — on simulated consulting tasks, with one model generation. The frontier moves.

### N5 — 18 tasks inside the frontier · +12.2% tasks completed · 25.1% faster · significantly higher quality
- **Value / unit:** 18 tasks · +12.2 percent tasks completed · 25.1 percent faster · quality "significantly improved" (**no percentage in the published abstract — do not write "+40%"**)
- **Denominator / population:** the 758 participants of N4
- **Comparison:** subjects using AI vs subjects not using AI
- **Task / setting:** 18 realistic knowledge tasks, creative through analytical, chosen to sit **inside** the frontier of AI capability
- **Source:** [28] — verbatim: *"For each one of a set of 18 realistic knowledge tasks within the frontier of AI capabilities ranging from creative to analytical tasks, subjects using AI outperformed those not using AI, completing 12.2% more tasks and completing them 25.1% more quickly on average while also delivering solutions of significantly improved quality."*
- **Boundary (verbatim, D.3 §28):** *"simulated consulting tasks, one participant population, and one model generation; the frontier moves."*
- **TH:** ในงาน 18 ชิ้นที่อยู่ *ในขอบเขต* ความสามารถของโมเดล กลุ่มที่ใช้ AI ทำงานเสร็จมากกว่า 12.2 เปอร์เซ็นต์ เร็วกว่า 25.1 เปอร์เซ็นต์ และคุณภาพดีขึ้นอย่างมีนัยสำคัญ
- **EN:** On the 18 tasks chosen to sit *inside* the capability frontier, the AI group completed 12.2 percent more tasks, 25.1 percent faster, at significantly better quality.

### N6 — −19% correct on the task outside the frontier
- **Value / unit:** 19 percent · relative likelihood of producing a correct solution
- **Denominator / population:** the same 758 participants
- **Comparison:** subjects using AI vs subjects without AI, on **one** deliberately selected task
- **Task / setting:** a complex managerial task selected to lie **outside** the frontier
- **Source:** [28] — verbatim: *"However, for a complex managerial task selected to be outside the frontier, subjects using AI were 19% less likely to produce correct solutions compared with those without AI"*
- **Boundary (verbatim, D.3 §28):** *"simulated consulting tasks, one participant population, and one model generation; the frontier moves."*
- **TH:** ส่วนงานบริหารที่ซับซ้อนหนึ่งงานซึ่งเลือกให้อยู่ *นอกขอบเขต* กลุ่มที่ใช้ AI มีโอกาสได้คำตอบถูกต้อง *น้อยกว่า* 19 เปอร์เซ็นต์ — งานเดียว ไม่ใช่ชุดงาน
- **EN:** On one complex managerial task selected to lie *outside* the frontier, the AI group was 19 percent *less* likely to produce a correct solution. One task, not a set.

### N7 — 453 professionals
- **Value / unit:** 453 · college-educated professionals
- **Denominator / population:** all participants in the preregistered online experiment
- **Comparison:** half randomly exposed to ChatGPT, half not
- **Task / setting:** occupation-specific, incentivised, **bounded mid-level professional writing tasks** (20–30 minutes each), online, one model generation
- **Source:** [27] — verbatim: *"In a preregistered online experiment, we assigned occupation-specific, incentivized writing tasks to 453 college-educated professionals and randomly exposed half of them to ChatGPT."*
- **Boundary (verbatim, D.3 §27):** *"short tasks and a specific model generation do not establish long-run organizational effects."*
- **TH:** การทดลองออนไลน์แบบลงทะเบียนล่วงหน้ากับมืออาชีพระดับปริญญาตรีขึ้นไป 453 คน สุ่มให้ครึ่งหนึ่งได้ใช้ ChatGPT บนงานเขียนเชิงวิชาชีพที่มีขอบเขตจำกัด
- **EN:** A preregistered online experiment gave incentivised, occupation-specific writing tasks to 453 college-educated professionals and randomly exposed half of them to ChatGPT.

### N8 — −40% time
- **Value / unit:** −40 percent · average time taken per task
- **Denominator / population:** the 453 participants of N7
- **Comparison:** exposed vs unexposed
- **Task / setting:** bounded writing tasks; a short-task result, not an organisational one
- **Source:** [27] — verbatim: *"The average time taken decreased by 40% and output quality rose by 18%."*
- **Boundary (verbatim, D.3 §27):** *"short tasks and a specific model generation do not establish long-run organizational effects."*

### N9 — +18% quality
- **Value / unit:** +18 percent · rated output quality
- **Denominator / population:** the 453 participants of N7
- **Comparison:** exposed vs unexposed, blind-rated by evaluators
- **Task / setting:** as N8
- **Source:** [27] — verbatim as N8. The book's own gloss (D.3 §27) reads: *"model access reduced average completion time by 40 percent and increased rated output quality by 18 percent on bounded writing tasks."*

### N10 — 106 experiments · 370 effect sizes
- **Value / unit:** 106 experimental studies · 370 effect sizes
- **Denominator / population:** studies published between **1 January 2020 and 30 June 2023**, found across the ACM Digital Library, Web of Science and the AIS eLibrary; each study had to report humans alone, AI alone **and** the combination
- **Comparison:** the three arms above, pooled as Hedges' g
- **Task / setting:** an interdisciplinary mix — decision tasks and creation tasks
- **Source:** [29] — verbatim: *"we addressed this question by conducting a preregistered systematic review and meta-analysis of 106 experimental studies reporting 370 effect sizes… for studies published between 1 January 2020 and 30 June 2023. Each study was required to include an original human-participants experiment that evaluated the performance of humans alone, AI alone and human–AI combinations."*
- **Boundary (verbatim, D.3 §29):** *"high heterogeneity and literature ending in June 2023 require local comparative testing."*
- **TH:** Meta-analysis ปี 2024 รวม 106 การทดลอง 370 ค่าขนาดผล จากงานที่ตีพิมพ์ระหว่าง 1 มกราคม 2020 ถึง 30 มิถุนายน 2023 โดยทุกงานต้องรายงานทั้งคนล้วน AI ล้วน และคนบวก AI
- **EN:** A 2024 meta-analysis pooled 106 experiments and 370 effect sizes from studies published between 1 January 2020 and 30 June 2023, each of which had to report human-only, AI-only **and** combined performance.

### N11 — g = −0.23: combined vs the *best* of either alone
- **Value / unit:** Hedges' g = −0.23 · 95% CI −0.39 to −0.07 (a small, negative pooled effect)
- **Denominator / population:** the 106 studies / 370 effect sizes of N10
- **Comparison:** human + AI vs **the better of** human-only or AI-only
- **Task / setting:** pooled across decision and creation tasks
- **Source:** [29] — verbatim: *"on average, human–AI combinations performed significantly worse than the best of humans or AI alone (Hedges' g = −0.23; 95% confidence interval, −0.39 to −0.07)"*
- **Boundary (verbatim, D.3 §29):** *"high heterogeneity and literature ending in June 2023 require local comparative testing."*
- **TH:** โดยเฉลี่ย ระบบคนบวก AI ทำได้ *แย่กว่า* ฝ่ายที่เก่งกว่าเมื่ออยู่เดี่ยว (g = −0.23; ช่วงเชื่อมั่น 95% −0.39 ถึง −0.07)
- **EN:** On average, human + AI performed significantly *worse* than the better of human-only or AI-only (g = −0.23; 95% CI −0.39 to −0.07).

### N12 — g = 0.64: combined vs the *human* alone
- **Value / unit:** Hedges' g = 0.64 · 95% CI 0.53 to 0.74 · t₉₈ = 11.87 · two-tailed P = 0.000 (a medium-to-large positive effect)
- **Denominator / population:** the subset of the same corpus with a human-alone baseline (t-statistic degrees of freedom 98)
- **Comparison:** human + AI vs **human alone**
- **Task / setting:** as N10
- **Source:** [29], **Results body, not the abstract** — verbatim: *"The human–AI systems performed significantly better than humans alone, and this pooled effect size was positive (g = 0.64; t₉₈ = 11.87; two-tailed P = 0.000; 95% CI, 0.53 to 0.74) and medium to large."* and *"the human–AI systems we analysed were, on average, better than humans alone but not better than both humans alone and AI alone."*
- **Boundary (verbatim, D.3 §29):** *"high heterogeneity and literature ending in June 2023 require local comparative testing."*
- **TH:** เทียบกับคนล้วน ระบบคนบวก AI ดีกว่าอย่างมีนัยสำคัญ (g = 0.64; ช่วงเชื่อมั่น 95% 0.53 ถึง 0.74) — สองข้อนี้เป็นจริงพร้อมกัน และนี่คือเหตุผลที่ต้องวัดทั้งสามแขน
- **EN:** Against the human-alone baseline the combination was significantly better (g = 0.64; 95% CI 0.53 to 0.74). Both statements hold at once — which is exactly why all three arms have to be measured.
- ⚠️ Cite as **body text**, not abstract. See Do-not-assert D-4.

### N13 — g = −0.27: decision tasks
- **Value / unit:** Hedges' g = −0.27 · 95% CI −0.44 to −0.10 · t₁₀₄ = −3.20 · two-tailed P = 0.002; the task-type moderation itself is F₁,₁₀₄ = 7.84, P = 0.006
- **Denominator / population:** the decision-task subgroup of the same corpus
- **Comparison:** human + AI vs the best of either alone, within decision tasks
- **Task / setting:** "tasks in which participants decided between a finite set of options"; contrasted with creation tasks, which showed significantly greater gains
- **Source:** [29] — verbatim: *"Among decision tasks—those in which participants decided between a finite set of options—the pooled effect size for human–AI synergy was significantly negative (g = −0.27; t₁₀₄ = −3.20; two-tailed P = 0.002; 95% CI, −0.44 to −0.10), which indicates performance losses from combining humans and AI."*
- **Boundary (verbatim, D.3 §29):** *"high heterogeneity and literature ending in June 2023 require local comparative testing."*
