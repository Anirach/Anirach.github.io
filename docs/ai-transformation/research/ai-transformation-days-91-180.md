### The three phases this post covers (pp. 51–52, verbatim)

**Days 91 to 120 release narrowly and learn from reality.** "Use canary or limited-group release
with support. Compare with baseline or control. Monitor outcome, quality, critical errors,
overrides, complaints, subgroup effects, cost, reviewer load, and environmental use. Rehearse the
kill path. Interview users and operators because workarounds expose defects dashboards miss. Stop
cases that lack value or cannot be controlled."

**Days 121 to 150 turn lessons into shared capability.** "Extract reusable services for model
access, context, evaluation, identity, tools, traces, data products, vendor clauses, impact
assessment, and training. Decide what is central and what remains in the domain. Establish intake,
funding, architecture, release, monitoring, and retirement. Do not build a large platform before the
first workflow reveals real requirements."

**Days 151 to 180 decide what earns scale.** "Review evidence with executives, operators, risk
functions, and worker representatives. For every initiative choose scale, reshape, hold, or stop.
Confirm realized value, residual risk, workforce effect, environmental cost, and learning rate.
Update policy and evaluation, close corrective actions, and select the next decisions that reuse the
core. Approve the next two-quarter roadmap and capability budget."

### Five operating principles (p. 52 EN / p. 53 TH) — never renumbered

1. **One learning loop before a large portfolio** — Depth before breadth. · สร้าง **Learning Loop** หนึ่งวงก่อน **Portfolio** ใหญ่ ลงลึกก่อนกระจาย
2. **Business and risk ownership cannot be delegated to the AI team** — Accountability follows the decision. · **Business** และ **Risk Ownership** โอนไปทีม **AI** ไม่ได้ Accountability ตาม Decision
3. **Instrument outcomes before scaling usage** — Login is not adoption and usage is not value. · วัด **Outcome** ก่อนเพิ่ม **Usage** Login ไม่ใช่ Adoption และ Usage ไม่ใช่ Value
4. **Build reusable capability from proven need** — Avoid platform speculation. · สร้าง **Reusable Capability** จากความต้องการที่พิสูจน์ หลีกเลี่ยง Platform Speculation
5. **Stop weak initiatives visibly and without stigma** — Portfolio discipline frees capacity for value. · หยุด **Initiative** ที่อ่อนอย่างโปร่งใส Portfolio Discipline คืนกำลังให้สิ่งมีคุณค่า

**Principle 3 is the one this post quotes in the 💡 blockquote** (per the spec's hook). The other
four are listed in prose. Do not renumber, do not re-translate: the Thai above is the book's own.
