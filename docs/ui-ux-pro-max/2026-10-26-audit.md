# UI/UX Pro Max audit — 2026-10-26

The owner asked to apply <https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill>. It is now
vendored at `.claude/skills/ui-ux-pro-max/` (see its `VENDORED.md`), and this is the first audit
it drove. House rules won every conflict (CLAUDE.md, "Complementary … skills"): the skill
supplied the checklist, and `page-design` / `a11y-perf` decided the fixes.

## What the skill recommended, and what was not taken

`search.py "personal portfolio consultant AI educator blog" --design-system` proposed
**Brutalism**, a zinc + blue-600 palette and **Caveat / Quicksand**. That would replace the
canonical 29-token `:root`, the Inter + Sarabun pair (Sarabun is load-bearing for Thai) and both
hero families. It was not applied. The skill's own priority table puts Style at 4 and Typography
at 6, below Accessibility (1), Touch (2), Performance (3) and Layout (5), so the audit started there.

## Method

- **axe-core 4.10.2** (`wcag2a/aa`, `wcag21a/aa`, `wcag22aa`, `best-practice`) on all **147** HTML
  files, in Chromium 153 at **375×900** with reduced motion. axe checks only the visible track
  (Thai), but both tracks share one stylesheet.
- **Playwright checks for the skill rules axe does not cover:** horizontal scroll at
  320/375/768/1440 in **both** language tracks; WCAG 2.5.8 target size (24px, with the spacing
  and inline-link exceptions); `cursor: pointer`; text under 12px; focus not obscured
  (WCAG 2.4.11, Shift+Tab from the bottom); and whether the language pill is visible when its
  checkbox has focus.
- Each failing colour was traced to its authored CSS rule over CDP
  (`CSS.getMatchedStylesForNode`), so only the rules that fail were edited.

## Fixed in this change

| Finding (skill rule) | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal scroll at 375px (`horizontal-scroll`, High) | 17 posts / 34 page-tracks | **0** |
| Horizontal scroll at 320px (WCAG 1.4.10 reflow) | 33 posts / 65 page-tracks | **0** |
| Language pill off-screen when its checkbox has focus (`focus-states`, WCAG 2.4.7) | 18 posts at 375×667, 1 at 1440×800 | **0** (135/135 at both sizes) |
| `color-contrast` (`color-contrast`, CRITICAL) | 308 nodes on 25 pages | 147 nodes on 15 pages |
| Decorative emoji read aloud (`no-emoji-icons`, aria-hidden) | 7 `.series-icon` + 8 inline emoji on news/projects/publications | 0 |

How:

1. **`.post-body { overflow-wrap: break-word; }`** added beside the `text-wrap` line in all 135
   posts and `page-design/assets/post-template.html`. The causes were long bare URLs as link text
   (the Good Life reference lists), long inline `<code>` paths (Hermes, Hermes Desktop), a
   bcrypt hash and an ASCII diagram typed into `<p>` tags (`deployment-hosting`). `<pre>` is
   unaffected: `white-space: pre` has no soft wraps. Table cells keep scrolling inside their
   wrappers, because `break-word` does not change min-content sizing.
2. **The 8 posts whose `.post-hero__tags` lacked `flex-wrap: wrap`** were snapped to the
   119-file majority. Two bespoke mobile grids (`beyond-plugins .stat-grid`,
   `claude-code-architecture .tool-grid`) became `repeat(2, minmax(0, 1fr))`, and
   `claude-code-architecture .tool-badge` now wraps.
3. **Language switch:** a progressive-enhancement line anchors the off-canvas checkbox to the pill
   (`anchor-name` / `position-anchor` / `anchor()`, `opacity: 0`, `pointer-events: none`), so
   focusing it scrolls the pill into view. The original `left: -9999px` line is unchanged and
   remains the fallback, which scrolls to the top of the page, as it always did.
4. **Contrast**, using the house's own remedies:
   - `--gray` as text on light grounds (2.13–2.56:1) became `--slate-light` (5.28–6.32:1). That
     covers `.flow-step__sub` in 17 posts, the `*-card__desc` / `.text-muted` /
     `.metric-card__detail` / `.promo-label` / `.stack-layer__label` rules, the inline greys in
     `automated-testing` and `cicd-pipeline`, and `obsidian-ai-jarvis .comparison__card.old h4`.
   - `code-quality .diff-comment` on `--code-bg`: `#526174` (2.31:1) became `--gray` (5.71:1).
   - Footer links in `git-branching` (`--blue-light`) and `obsidian-ai-jarvis` (`--purple-light`),
     both 3.93:1 on navy, became `--gold` (5.73:1), like every other post footer.
   - `database-sql .post-nav__dir`: `--emerald` (2.51:1) became `--blue`, the 20-file majority.
   - `projects .card__tag--research`: `--cyan` (2.21:1) became `#0e7490`, the 700 shade its Live
     and Workshop siblings already use.
5. **`aria-hidden="true"`** on the `.series-icon` spans of `news/`, `projects/` and
   `publications/`, matching `blog/`, `thoughts/` and `books/`. The emoji inside the
   projects status tags (🟢 🔬 🎓) and the news 📘 heading are wrapped the same way.

Gates: `check_site.py` 0 new / 0 known, 0 warn (same as before); `check_visibility.py --strict`
0 fail; `check-news-sync.py` PASS; `build_series.py --check` OK for all six generated series
(they lift their `<style>` from the edited skeleton posts, so new posts inherit both lines).

## Open — the owner's call

Ranked by the skill's priorities. Counts are axe nodes / pages at 375px after this change.

1. **Status colours inside the old HTML-diagram components: 147 / 15 posts.** Green, red and amber
   text on their own tints, and white on bright badges. Worst offenders: `web-architecture` 30,
   `database-sql` 24, `authentication-authorization` 23, `api-request-lifecycle` 17,
   `cicd-pipeline` 12, `idle-self-improvement` 10, `gitops-argocd` 7, `automated-testing` 6,
   `git-branching` 4, and 1–3 each in 6 more. Darkening each hue to its 700/800 shade would pass,
   but every component carries its own palette. `page-design` anti-pattern 1 also says these
   inline diagrams should become PNGs when touched, which is the better fix and a larger job.
2. **`scrollable-region-focusable`: 467 / 75 pages.** `.table-wrapper` and `<pre>` that scroll
   sideways on a phone are not in the tab order. Chrome ≥130 and Firefox now make scroll
   containers keyboard-focusable themselves; Safari does not. The fix is `tabindex="0"` plus
   `role="region"` and an `aria-label` on each wrapper, in both tracks and in
   `build_series.py`'s output. That would add a tab stop on desktop too, where most of these do
   not scroll, so it is a trade-off to choose deliberately.
3. **Small labels: 0.7–0.72rem (11.2–11.5px)** on tags, kickers, eyebrows and `.ref-tag` on every
   catalog and most posts, many in Thai. Body text is 16–17px everywhere, so the skill's
   `readable-font-size` rule passes. Thai with stacked tone marks is still hard to read at 11px.
   Raising the floor to 0.75rem would be a type-scale change, which `page-design` §2 reserves for
   the owner.
4. **Emoji as icons (`no-emoji-icons`).** The landing page has none; it uses inline SVG. The
   catalog `.series-tile__icon` / `.series-icon` and the books/news/projects/publications section
   icons are emoji, all now `aria-hidden`. 356 of 2362 post `<h2>` still carry an emoji
   (`page-design` anti-pattern 13). Swapping to inline SVG like the landing page would be a visual
   identity change.
5. **Already on the house backlog, confirmed by axe:**
   - `heading-order`: 43 nodes / 31 posts (a11y-perf rule 8).
   - `region`: 135× `#langSwitch` outside a landmark, plus 23× `.post-nav`. The first needs `:has()`
     in place of the sibling selector; for the second, INV-04c requires a `<div>` (a11y-perf R6).
   - `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`: 40× `<aside>` inside `<main>`. This is valid HTML and
     only an axe best practice.
   - `empty-table-header`: 24 / 16 posts, first-column `<th>` left blank.
   - `link-in-text-block`: 1, a non-underlined link in `obsidian-ai-jarvis`.
6. **`deployment-hosting`'s ASCII diagram** sits in `<p>` tags and has already lost its box-drawing
   layout. It now wraps instead of widening the page, but it should be redrawn as a PNG
   (anti-pattern 1).

Clean, with nothing to do: WCAG 2.5.8 target size (axe `target-size`, all 147 pages) and, on the
9 section pages plus 6 sample posts, `cursor: pointer` on every control and focus never hidden
by the sticky nav or `.blog-jump`. Body text is 16–17px throughout.
