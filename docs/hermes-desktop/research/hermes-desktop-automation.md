## Do not assert

Things I looked for and could not verify, and therefore left out or hedged:

- **The Desktop Routines dialog's button labels.** The docs describe the schedule picker and the Advanced field but never name a "New" or "Save" button. The procedure therefore says "create the job from the Routines pane" instead of naming a control. The only button label in the post, **Trigger now**, comes from PR #70638's title and body [12].
