Approach

Three ways to work together รูปแบบการทำงานร่วมกัน

Pick the one that fits the problem. Most engagements start with a conversation and grow from there.

Advise

Strategy sessions, diagnostics and a standing advisory seat beside your leadership team.

Best for a decision with a deadline

Teach

Workshops, executive briefings and keynotes — in Thai or English, for ten people or a full hall.

Best for getting a whole team on the same page

Build

A working prototype or reference implementation, built with your team so they own the result.

Best for proving it works before you scale it

  1. 01

    Listen

    A first conversation about the decision in front of you. No slides.

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    Your data, systems and people — and an honest name for what is really in the way.

  3. 03

    Design & build

    Architecture, governance and a working pilot, with clear gates for quality and cost.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    Documentation, training and tests, so your team can run it without me.

About

A teacher, a dean, a researcher

ผมสอนหนังสือมาสามสิบสี่ปี งานวิจัยตอนนี้ถามคำถามเดียว — AI ช่วยให้หมอตัดสินใจได้ดีขึ้นจริงไหม — และระหว่างนั้นผมก็เขียนหนังสือถึงคนทุกคน

I have taught for thirty-four years. My research now asks one question — can AI actually help a doctor decide? — and alongside it I write books for everyone.

That mix is what I bring to an engagement: a teacher’s patience for explaining, a dean’s sense of how institutions really change, and a researcher’s insistence on evidence.
