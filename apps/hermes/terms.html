Hermes Hostinger Terms of Use

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Hermes Hostinger is a personal assistant integration operated by Anirach Mingkhwan for accounts and data he is authorized to manage. It is not a public service, and this website does not provide registration or grant access to the bot.

Authorized use

The owner must authorize each Google account through Google’s consent flow and use the assistant only for data and actions he has permission to manage. University and other organizational policies continue to apply to organization-managed accounts.

Requests to send email, invite attendees, share files, or delete data must identify the intended action and relevant recipients or items. Connecting an account alone does not instruct the assistant to perform those actions.

Owner control and review

The owner controls bot access, connected accounts, and requested actions. AI-generated summaries, drafts, dates, and other outputs may be incomplete or incorrect. The owner should review consequential outputs and supply essential details before an action is taken.

Service availability

The integration depends on Google APIs, Hermes, the hosting server, the configured OpenAI service, Telegram, and network availability. API quotas, provider policies, outages, expired authorizations, and configuration changes may interrupt it. Availability and uninterrupted operation are not guaranteed.

Privacy and third-party services

The Privacy Policy explains the integration’s data practices. Use of connected services remains subject to their applicable terms and account policies. These terms do not grant rights to other people’s data or override those services’ rules.

Disconnecting or ending use

The owner can stop the bot, remove a connection from the server, or revoke Google access through Google Account connections. Previously stored or delivered information must be deleted separately where applicable, as described in the Privacy Policy.
